# Code of conduct

This repository's issues are a place to report problems and suggest improvements to Luminara.
Everyone taking part is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent,
- keep to the topic of the issue,
- not harass, insult or demean anyone, and not post anyone's private information,
- not post pirated books, or links to them.

The maintainers may edit, hide or lock comments, close issues, and block accounts that don't
follow these rules. To report a problem with someone's behavior, contact the maintainers through
the [Caemlyn organization page](https://github.com/caemlyn).
